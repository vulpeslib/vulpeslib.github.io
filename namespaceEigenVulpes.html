inlineprivate

Compare two dense matrices for approximate equality.

Matrices are considered equal if their dimensions match and the norm of their difference is within tolerance of the second matrix's norm. On mismatch, a diagnostic message is printed to stdout.

Parameters
mat1First matrix.
mat2Second (reference) matrix.
toleranceRelative tolerance applied to mat2's norm.
Returns
true if the matrices match in size and are numerically close.

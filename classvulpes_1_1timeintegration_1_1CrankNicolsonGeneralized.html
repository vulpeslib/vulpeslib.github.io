Detailed Description

Crank-Nicolson solver for linear and nonlinear problems of the form.

M du/dt + A u + N(u) u = g(u) + F(t)

A is the static linear operator (any linear reaction must be added to it, e.g. A - alpha M for Fisher-Kolmogorov), N(u) a solution dependent matrix and g(u) a solution dependent vector. Every step solves

(M + h/2 (A + N*)) u_{n+1} = (M - h/2 (A + N*)) u_{n}

  • h/2 (F_{n} + F_{n+1}) + h g*

with N* = N(u*), g* = g(u*).

  • linear problem: the LHS is factorized only once;
  • Picard iterations disabled (default): u* is the Adams-Bashforth extrapolation 3/2 u_{n} - 1/2 u_{n-1}, one solve per step;
  • Picard iterations enabled: after the first solve the step is repeated with u* = (u_{n} + u_{n+1}^k) / 2 until convergence (fully implicit CN).

All the terms are assembled by the user through a single AssembleFunc, called once per step (and once per Picard iteration) with the terms needed in that call, so that F(t_{n+1}), N(u*) and g(u*) can be integrated in the same loop on the cells. The requested terms are zeroed before the call and compressed afterwards by the solver. The matrix has the sparsity pattern of M + A, entries outside that pattern are not allowed. Alternatively, a ForceFunc and an optional LinearizeFunc can be passed: they are called one after the other (two loops on the cells).

Detailed Description

Leapfrog solver, in velocity Verlet form, for linear second order problems of the form.

M d^2u/dt^2 + A u = F(t)

With w = M du/dt, every step computes

M u_{n+1} = (M - h^2/2 A) u_{n} + h w_{n} + h^2/2 F_{n} w_{n+1} = w_{n} - h/2 A (u_{n} + u_{n+1}) + h/2 (F_{n} + F_{n+1})

The scheme is explicit and second order: the only system to solve has the matrix M, factorized once. For A symmetric positive definite it is stable for h sqrt(lambda_max) < 2, with lambda_max the largest eigenvalue of M^{-1} A, and without forcing the energy (w^T M^{-1} w + u^T A u) / 2 oscillates with an amplitude of order h^2, without drifting.

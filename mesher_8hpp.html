Detailed Description

VulPoly centroidal Voronoi mesh generation for 2D and 3D domains.

Centroidal relaxation, boundary reflection, fixed-point handling, and short-edge removal are based on PolyMesher. Vulpes adds boundary-vertex correction and adapts Voronoi cells to its mesh data structures.

Reference
Talischi et al., "PolyMesher: a general-purpose mesh generator for polygonal elements written in Matlab," Structural and Multidisciplinary Optimization 45, 309-328 (2012). DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00158-011-0706-z.
